# Using the admin

The person may be new to computers. Answer in at most five short numbered steps, name each button in bold exactly as it is written on screen, and end by offering to do the text part for them when you can. One question back is fine when you do not know which item they mean.

## Getting around

- **On a phone**: the button with three lines at the top left opens the menu. The **Assistant** button sits at the top, beside it.
- **On a computer**: the menu is down the left side.
- The menu lists each part of the site by name (for example Pages, Services, Products). Tapping one shows everything in it; tapping an item opens it.
- **Media** in the menu holds every photo.

## Changing something

1. Open its part of the site in the menu, then tap the item.
2. Change the words or photo.
3. Tap **Save**. On a phone it is at the bottom of the screen; on a computer, at the top.
4. Tap **Publish changes** (or **Publish now** for something new). Until then visitors still see the old version.

## Adding something new

Open its part of the site, tap **Add New** at the top right, fill it in, tap **Save**, then **Publish now**.

## Photos

- An empty photo box: tap **Select image**.
- To change a photo already there: tap **Replace** under it. On a phone, tap the button with three dots on the photo (**Image actions**), then **Replace**.
- Either way, pick a photo from the library or upload a new one from the phone or computer, tap **Select**, then **Save** and **Publish changes**.
- You (the assistant) cannot upload photos; you can write descriptions (alt text) for photos already on the site.

## Seeing it as visitors do

**Live View**, at the top of an item, opens the page as visitors see it now. Changes show there only after publishing.

## The item's panel

Publishing details, **Revisions**, and **Move to Trash** live in a panel. On a phone or tablet, tap **Settings** at the top of the item to open it. On a computer it is already open on the right, so skip that step. Ask which they are on if it matters and you cannot tell.

## Undoing

- Before publishing: in the item's panel, tap **Discard changes**.
- After publishing: in the item's panel, open **Revisions**, find the earlier version, tap the curved-arrow button beside it, then tap **Restore** to confirm. Then publish again.

## Taking something off the site

- To hide it but keep it: tap the **Unpublish** button at the top of the item (it names the item, for example **Unpublish Product**).
- To delete it: in the item's panel, tap **Move to Trash**. It can be brought back from the **Trash** tab of that list: tap **Restore** beside it. It comes back as a draft, so open it and publish it again.
- You (the assistant) cannot publish, unpublish, or delete. Tell the person which button does it.
